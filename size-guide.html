Size Guide

How to measure

Bust: measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape flat under your arms.

Waist: measure around the narrowest part of your natural waist, above the belly button.

Hip: measure around the fullest part of your hips, with your feet together.

Measure over light clothing, and keep the tape snug but not tight. Between sizes, we suggest the larger size for knitwear and the smaller for wrap styles. Accessories such as scarves, earrings and tote bags are one size.
