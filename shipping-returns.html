Shipping and Returns

Delivery

Orders are packed within 1 to 2 working days. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days and costs $5. Delivery is free on orders over $75.

We deliver to the address you give at checkout, so please check it once before you place the order. If a parcel comes back to us because the address was wrong, we will write to you and arrange a new delivery.

Returns, 14 days

Yes, you can return an item. You have 14 days from delivery to return unworn items with tags on. Pieces must come back in the condition they arrived in, with no marks, scents or wear beyond trying on at home.

Sale items can be exchanged for size or store credit. For hygiene reasons, earrings can only be returned if the seal is unopened.

How to return

Write to us first at hello@threadthistle.example.com with your order number, and we will confirm the return address. Pack the piece, send it back, and keep your posting receipt. Once the piece arrives and passes a quick check, your refund goes back to the way you paid within 5 working days.

Exchanges

Need a different size? Tell us in the same email and we will hold the new size for you where stock allows, then send it once your return is on its way.
