Good questions

FAQ

Sizes, delivery, returns and fabric care, answered plainly.

How do I find my size?

Check the size guide page and measure yourself before ordering. Between sizes, we suggest the larger size for knitwear and the smaller for wrap styles. Every product page also carries a fit note.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are packed within 1 to 2 working days. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days and costs $5, and it is free on orders over $75.

Can I return an item?

Yes. You have 14 days from delivery to return unworn items with tags on. Sale items can be exchanged for size or store credit. See Shipping and Returns for the full policy.

What fabrics do you use?

We favour cotton, linen, wool blends and viscose. The full fabric content is printed on every product page and on the care label.

Do you restock sold out sizes?

Popular pieces are restocked when fabric is available. Join the newsletter or use the notify button on the product page to hear first.

Is checkout secure?

Yes, checkout runs on a secure connection. This demo store uses test payments only, so no real card is ever charged here.

Still stuck? Write to us and we will help.
