About the boutique

Our Story

Thread and Thistle began with a simple frustration: pretty clothes that fell apart after three washes. We set out to make a small boutique where every piece is chosen for fabric first and trend second. Our collections are small on purpose. Each dress, knit and accessory has to earn its place, mix with the rest of the rail and feel good on a normal Tuesday, not just in photos. We hope you find pieces here that stay in your wardrobe for years.

Fabric first

We choose natural and long lasting fabrics wherever we can, and we write the true fabric content on every product page. If a piece is delicate, we say so.

Sizing, honestly

If a piece runs small, we say that too. Every product page carries a fit note, and the size guide shows bust, waist and hip in inches so you can measure before you order. No surprises when your parcel arrives.
